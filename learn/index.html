How to style your Instagram captions

Four short guides, each a few steps long, in plain language: bold and italic text, bullet points, divider lines, and the character limit. They work the same for a bio, a YouTube description or a LinkedIn post. Start with whichever you need.

I·Styling

Bold, italic and fontsMake a few words stand out, or set the whole caption in a different font. It takes a selection and one tap.

II·Lists

Lists and your own bulletsTurn a few lines into a tidy list, then swap the dot for a flower, an arrow, or anything you can type.

III·Dividers

Dividers you designA divider is a decorative line between parts of a caption. It is made of ordinary characters, so it pastes like any other text.

IV·And the rest

Saving, counting and dark modeKeep captions to reuse, see how much room is left before the platform cuts you off, and pick the look you prefer.
